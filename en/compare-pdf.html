Compare PDF

Find out in seconds what changed between two versions of a contract, a proposal or a paper, without reading them end to end.

  • No file uploads
  • Free, no sign-up
  • Works in your browser

How to compare two PDFs in 3 steps

  1. Choose the original PDF and the new version.
  2. Press "Compare".
  3. Review each change: additions in green and removals struck through in red. Download the report if you need it.

Your files never leave your device

Unlike other online tools, DocuSmart doesn't upload your documents to any server: everything is processed inside your browser. You can use it with contracts, invoices or ID documents with complete peace of mind. Read our privacy policy (in Spanish).

Frequently asked questions

What kind of changes does it detect?

Changes in the text: words, numbers or paragraphs added, removed or modified. If only the line breaks or spacing changed, it doesn't count as a change. Changes in images or layout are not detected.

Does it work with scanned documents?

A scan is an image and has no text to compare. Run each version through the OCR tool first and compare the searchable PDFs it creates.

Does it matter if the pages don't match?

No. The whole document is compared, so if a new paragraph pushes everything to the next page, only that paragraph is marked. Each change shows which page it is on in each version.

Are my documents uploaded to the internet?

No. The comparison happens inside your browser.
