PDF to PDF/A

Convert your documents to the standard archival format so they look the same many years from now, without depending on external fonts or software.

How to convert a PDF to PDF/A in 3 steps

  1. Choose the PDF.
  2. Press "Convert to PDF/A".
  3. The PDF/A-2b is downloaded and we tell you what was adjusted to meet the standard.

Frequently asked questions

What is PDF/A?

It's the version of PDF designed for archiving (ISO 19005): the file contains its fonts, colour profile and metadata, and can't depend on anything external. Many procedures with public bodies, courts and universities require it.

What level of PDF/A does it create?

PDF/A-2b, the most widely used level: it guarantees the document will look the same in the future. We validate it with veraPDF, the PDF Association's reference validator.

Does anything in my document change?

Missing fonts are embedded and things the standard doesn't allow are removed, such as scripts, attachments or sounds. If a page uses print colours (CMYK) or fonts that aren't in the file, that page is saved as an image.

Is the file uploaded?

No. The conversion happens in your browser.
