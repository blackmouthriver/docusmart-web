Split PDF

Extract the pages you need or put every page in its own file, right in your browser.

  • No file uploads
  • Free, no sign-up
  • Works in your browser

How to split a PDF in 3 steps

  1. Choose the PDF you want to split.
  2. Tap the pages you want to keep, type a range such as "1-3, 5" or choose "Each page separately".
  3. Download the result: a PDF with the chosen pages or a .zip with one page per file.

Your files never leave your device

Unlike other online tools, DocuSmart doesn't upload your documents to any server: everything is processed inside your browser. You can use it with contracts, invoices or ID documents with complete peace of mind. Read our privacy policy (in Spanish).

Frequently asked questions

How do I extract only some pages?

Tap them in the thumbnails or type a range, for example "2-4, 7", or "5-" to go from page 5 to the end.

Is the original PDF changed?

No. A new file is always created; the original stays the same on your device.

Is it safe for private documents?

Yes. The file isn't uploaded to the internet: the whole process happens in your browser.
