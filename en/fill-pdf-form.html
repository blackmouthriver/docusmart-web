Fill in PDF form

Complete applications, registrations and fillable PDF forms without printing them or installing software, seeing on the page where each answer goes.

  • No file uploads
  • Free, no sign-up
  • Works in your browser

How to fill in a PDF form in 3 steps

  1. Choose the PDF form: DocuSmart finds its fields and marks them on the page.
  2. Type your answers; tapping a box on the page opens its field, and vice versa.
  3. If you are going to send it, tick "Flatten" so the answers become fixed, and press "Fill in form".

Your files never leave your device

Unlike other online tools, DocuSmart doesn't upload your documents to any server: everything is processed inside your browser. You can use it with contracts, invoices or ID documents with complete peace of mind. Read our privacy policy (in Spanish).

Frequently asked questions

Which forms work?

PDFs with fillable fields (AcroForm), which are the vast majority: text boxes, checkboxes, options, drop-down lists and lists. If a PDF has no fields, you can write on top with "Edit PDF".

What does flattening mean?

It makes the answers a fixed part of the document: they look the same in any reader and nobody can change them without leaving a trace. Without flattening, the form stays editable.

Can I keep filling it in later?

Yes, if you don't flatten it: open the saved PDF again here or in any reader and your answers will still be in the fields.

Is my data uploaded to the internet?

No. The form and your answers are processed inside your browser.
