Reorder PDF pages

Move each page of your PDF to the right place and remove the ones you don't need, with a preview of every page. The document never leaves your device.

  • No file uploads
  • Free, no sign-up
  • Works in your browser

How to reorder PDF pages in 3 steps

  1. Choose or drag in the PDF.
  2. Move each thumbnail with its arrows and remove extra pages with the trash button.
  3. Press "Apply new order" and download the PDF.

Your files never leave your device

Unlike other online tools, DocuSmart doesn't upload your documents to any server: everything is processed inside your browser. You can use it with contracts, invoices or ID documents with complete peace of mind. Read our privacy policy (in Spanish).

Frequently asked questions

Can I delete pages while reordering?

Yes. Each thumbnail has a button to remove it; the final PDF only includes the pages you keep.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens inside your browser: the document never leaves your device.

Is quality lost when reordering?

No. Pages are copied as they are, with their original text, images and size.

What if I want to combine pages from several PDFs?

Merge them first with the Merge PDF tool, then reorder the pages of the result.
