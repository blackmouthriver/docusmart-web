Adjust image

Brighten a dark photo, make the colors pop, turn it black and white or straighten the horizon. See the result instantly and apply it to all your photos at once.

  • No file uploads
  • Free, no sign-up
  • Works in your browser

How to adjust a photo's brightness and color in 3 steps

  1. Choose one or more images (JPG, PNG, WebP or HEIC).
  2. Move the brightness, contrast, saturation or angle, or tick "Black and white", while watching the preview.
  3. Press "Apply adjustments" and download the result; with several images, all together in a ZIP.

Your files never leave your device

Unlike other online tools, DocuSmart doesn't upload your documents to any server: everything is processed inside your browser. You can use it with contracts, invoices or ID documents with complete peace of mind. Read our privacy policy (in Spanish).

Frequently asked questions

How do I brighten a dark photo?

Raise the brightness and, if it looks dull, the contrast a little. With "Compare with the original" you see the before and after in the same preview.

What is straightening for?

To fix crooked photos, like a tilted horizon or a document photographed at an angle. The photo is rotated up to 15° and trimmed a little so no empty corners are left.

Can I turn several photos black and white?

Yes. Choose them all, tick "Black and white" and they are converted with the same settings; they download together in a ZIP.

Does the photo lose quality?

It keeps its size and format (JPG, PNG or WebP; iPhone HEIC photos are saved as JPG). Only straightening trims the edges a little. Hidden photo data (EXIF), such as the GPS location, is also removed.

Are my photos uploaded to the internet?

No. The adjustments happen inside your browser: your photos never leave your device.
