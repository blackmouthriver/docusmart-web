How to remove the location and hidden data from a photo

A phone photo can store the exact place where it was taken, and a document, the name of whoever wrote it. Before posting or sending them, see what they carry and erase it.

4 min readUpdated on November 27, 2026

What hidden data (metadata) is

It's data stored inside the file that you can't see in the image or on the pages. A photo can store the GPS location, the make and model of the phone, the serial number of the camera and the exact date and time. A PDF or a Word document can store the author, the company, the program it was made with and the creation and modification dates.

Anyone who receives the file can see it with any viewer. With a photo of your home, for example, the location leads to your address.

Step by step

Open Remove metadata and choose your photos or documents: JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, TIFF, MP4 and MOV videos, PDF, Word, Excel or PowerPoint. You'll instantly see the list of Hidden data found in each one, as in the image; if a photo stores the location, it appears in red with a warning.

Press Remove metadata. You'll see how much data each file had before and how much remains after, and you download the clean copy (or a ZIP, if there were several).

Only the orientation of the photos is kept, so they don't appear rotated. The image isn't recompressed: it doesn't lose quality.

In PDFs and Office documents

In PDFs, the Also remove comments, attachments and JavaScript option also deletes notes with their author's name, attached files and code that runs when the file is opened. Turn it off if you need to keep the comments.

In Word, Excel and PowerPoint, the author, the company and the dates are removed. Names that appear in comments and tracked changes have to be removed in the program itself.

If the private data is visible

Removing metadata doesn't change what you see. If what you want to hide is an ID number, a signature or an address that appears on the pages of a PDF, use Redact PDF, which covers it and removes it from the file.

It's done on your device

Everything happens in your browser: your photos and documents aren't uploaded to any server. That way you don't leave copies of a private file just when you're trying to protect it.

Quick summary

  1. Open Remove metadata. Go to docusmart.online/en/remove-metadata and choose photos or documents.
  2. Check what they store. Look at the list of hidden data in each file, such as the GPS location.
  3. Erase it. Press Remove metadata and check that none is left.
  4. Download. Download the clean copy, ready to share.

Frequently asked questions

Does WhatsApp remove the location from photos?

When you send them as photos, WhatsApp recompresses them and removes most of the data. But if you send them as documents, by email or upload them to other sites, they can arrive with the location.

Does the photo lose quality?

No. Only the hidden data is removed; the image stays exactly the same.

What data is kept?

Only the orientation of the photos, so they don't appear rotated. Everything else is erased.
