How to zip files and open a ZIP or RAR

A ZIP combines many files into one: easier to send by email and sometimes lighter. Here you create and open them in your browser, without installing any software.

4 min readUpdated on November 27, 2026

Creating a ZIP, step by step

Open Compress files (ZIP and 7Z) and add the files (of any type) or a whole folder with Add a folder. Enter the name of the ZIP and choose the compression: Fast, Normal or Maximum.

Press Create ZIP. You'll see your files on one side and the ZIP on the other, with how much everything weighed before and how much it weighs now.

Photos, videos and PDFs are already compressed, so they barely get smaller in a ZIP. What does shrink a lot are Word documents, spreadsheets and text files.

With a password or in parts

  • Protect with a password (AES-256): nobody can open the ZIP without the password. In ZIP the password can't contain accented letters or ñ; if you need them, choose the 7Z format.
  • Split into parts: if your email won't accept such a large file, you split it into several (.001, .002…). To open it you need all the parts, with DocuSmart, 7-Zip or WinRAR.
  • 7Z: compresses more than ZIP, but on some computers you have to install a program to open it. ZIP opens on any computer and phone without installing anything.

Opening a ZIP, RAR or 7Z

To get out what a compressed file contains, use Extract RAR, ZIP and 7Z. It opens ZIP, RAR, 7Z, TAR, GZ, BZ2 and XZ, including password-protected ones and ones split into parts.

You'll see the list of what's inside, with its folders. You can preview a file before downloading it, extract only the ones you choose or download them all together.

If what you want is a smaller PDF

Putting a PDF in a ZIP barely shrinks it. To really make a PDF smaller, run it through Compress PDF, which reduces the images inside it.

Your files don't leave your device

Compressing and extracting happen in your browser's memory: nothing is uploaded to the internet. With very large files (several GB) it may take a while or run out of memory; in that case, split them into several ZIPs.

Quick summary

  1. Add the files. Go to docusmart.online/en/zip-files and add files or a folder.
  2. Choose the options. Enter the name, the compression and, if you want, a password.
  3. Create the ZIP. Press Create ZIP and see how much the size went down.
  4. Download. Download the ZIP or share it.

Frequently asked questions

Can I open a RAR without WinRAR?

Yes. Extract RAR, ZIP and 7Z opens RAR, ZIP, 7Z and other formats in the browser, without installing anything. To create a new one, use ZIP or 7Z.

Why is my ZIP almost the same size?

Because photos, videos and PDFs are already compressed. A ZIP is for combining them into a single file, not so much for shrinking them.

Is it safe to add a password?

Yes. It's encrypted with AES-256 and the password isn't stored anywhere: if you forget it, there's no way to recover it.
