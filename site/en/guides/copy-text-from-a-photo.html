How to copy the text from a photo or a screenshot

A photo of a receipt, a screenshot of a chat or a sign: the text is right in front of you, but you can't copy it. Here is how to get it out in seconds, without typing it by hand.

3 min readUpdated on November 26, 2026

Step by step

Open Image to text (OCR) and choose the photo. JPG, PNG, WebP and iPhone HEIC photos all work. If it's a screenshot, you don't even need to save it: copy it and paste it into the page with Ctrl+V.

Pick the language under Document language and press Recognize text. In a few seconds the text appears in a box you can correct. From there you copy it with one tap or download it as .txt or Word (.docx).

If the text mixes your language with English words (product names, technical terms), tick "The document also has English text": it gets those words right more often.

Several photos into one document

If you photographed a document with several sheets, choose all the photos at once. The text from all of them comes out together, in order, and you download it as a single Word file. It's the fastest way to digitize a few printed pages someone lent you.

How to take the photo so it reads well

Recognition depends on the photo. A few details make all the difference:

  • Good light and no shadows: your phone's shadow on the paper is the most common cause of errors.
  • Straight on and level: the less tilted, the better.
  • Make the text look big: move closer until the sheet fills the photo.
  • Sharp: if the photo came out blurry, take it again.

If the result looks doubtful, DocuSmart warns you with the confidence percentage so you can check the text before using it. Handwriting is rarely recognized well: the tool is designed for printed or on-screen text.

Photo or scanned PDF?

If what you have is a scanned PDF with several pages, or you want to keep the document as it looks and also be able to search for words, use OCR: recognize text, which creates a searchable PDF. And if you need to hand in the photos as a document, put them together first with Image to PDF.

Your photos stay on your device

The text is recognized in your browser: the images aren't uploaded to any server. You can use it with photos of documents, receipts or conversations without leaving copies anywhere else.

Quick summary

  1. Open Image to text. Go to docusmart.online/en/image-to-text and choose the photo, or paste a screenshot with Ctrl+V.
  2. Pick the language. Choose the language of the text in the image.
  3. Recognize the text. Press Recognize text and check the result in the box.
  4. Copy or download. Copy it with one tap or download it as .txt or Word.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work with iPhone photos?

Yes. HEIC photos open directly, without converting them first.

Does it recognize handwriting?

Rarely well. It works with printed or on-screen text.

Can I correct the text before copying it?

Yes. The text appears in an editable box: fix whatever you need and then copy or download it.
