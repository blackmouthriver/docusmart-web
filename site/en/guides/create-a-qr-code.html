How to create a QR code for free

A QR code takes people to your page, opens a WhatsApp chat or connects them to the Wi-Fi without typing anything. Create it in seconds; it doesn't expire or depend on any server.

4 min readUpdated on November 27, 2026

Step by step

Open Create and read QR codes and choose what it will store: URL, Text, Email, Phone, WhatsApp, SMS, Location, Wi-Fi, Contact or Event. Enter the details (for a link, the full address with https://).

Press Generate QR. The code appears right away and you can download it as PNG, as SVG (vector, prints sharp at any size), as PDF (10 cm), copy it as an image or print it.

Before printing a hundred flyers, scan the QR code with your phone to check that it opens what you expect.

What each type can do

  • WhatsApp: enter the number with the country code (for example, 1 for the US or 57 for Colombia) and, if you want, a message. When scanned, the chat opens with the message already written.
  • Wi-Fi: the network name, the security type and the password. Your guests connect without you having to read it out.
  • Contact: name, phone, email and company. When scanned, the phone offers to save it to the contacts.
  • Location: an address or coordinates that open in the map.
  • Event: title, place, start and end, to add it to the calendar.

Give it your style

In Customize design you change the color of the code and the background (or make it transparent to place it on your design), put your logo in the center and choose the image size. If the colors have low contrast, DocuSmart warns you, because a camera might not be able to read it.

With Protected, the content of the QR code is encrypted with a password (AES-256): only someone who has it can open it, in DocuSmart. And with Several QR codes from a list you generate many at once, one per line or from a CSV, for example for a restaurant's tables or inventory labels.

To read a QR code

If what you need is to read one, use QR code reader: with the camera, from a photo or a screenshot, or from a PDF. Before opening a link it shows you where it leads and warns you if it looks deceptive.

Your QR code doesn't expire

The code is generated in your browser and stores the data directly, without going through an intermediate link. That's why it doesn't expire, has no scan limit and nobody else sees what you put in it. The QR codes you create stay in History, only in your browser.

Quick summary

  1. Choose the type. Go to docusmart.online/en/qr-code-generator and choose URL, WhatsApp, Wi-Fi or another type.
  2. Enter the details. Add the link, the number or the network details.
  3. Generate the QR code. Press Generate QR and, if you want, change the color or add your logo.
  4. Download it. Download it as PNG, SVG or PDF, or print it.

Frequently asked questions

Does the QR code expire?

No. The data goes inside the code itself, without an intermediate link, so it works forever and with no scan limit.

Which format should I use for printing?

SVG: it's vector and looks sharp at any size, from a business card to a poster. There's also a 10 cm PDF.

Can I put my logo on the QR code?

Yes, in Customize design. The logo sits in a circle in the center and the code can still be read.
