How to resize a photo

A phone photo is thousands of pixels across: too much for a form, a web page or a cover image. Here you bring it to the size you need, without distorting it.

4 min readUpdated on November 27, 2026

Step by step

Open Resize image and choose one or several photos (JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, GIF or BMP). Then pick How to resize:

  • By percentage: for example, 50% makes the photo half as wide and half as tall.
  • By pixels: enter the width, the height or both. With Keep the aspect ratio on, one side is enough: the other is calculated automatically.
  • Social media: exact sizes for Instagram, WhatsApp stories, Facebook or LinkedIn covers and YouTube thumbnails.
  • In centimeters: for printing, at the resolution you choose.

Press Resize and you'll see the photo before and after, with its file size. If you chose several, they're saved together in a ZIP.

Keep it from getting distorted

A photo looks stretched when you change the width and the height in different proportions. That's why Keep the aspect ratio is on by default: if you enter both sides, the photo fits inside that size without stretching. In Social media, the image fills the exact size and whatever is left over is trimmed evenly from the center.

Making a photo smaller never makes it look less sharp, but making it bigger doesn't add detail: a small photo enlarged to twice its size looks blurry.

If what you want is to crop it or make it lighter

Resizing isn't the same as cropping. To remove an edge, frame a person or make the photo square, use Crop image: free cropping or fixed ratios such as 1:1, 4:3 or 16:9.

And if a form requires the photo to be under a certain size (for example, 200 KB), Compress image lowers the quality just enough for it to fit, and only reduces the pixels if that's not enough.

Commonly requested sizes

  • For a web page or a blog: between 1200 and 1920 pixels wide is more than enough; anything larger just makes the page load slowly.
  • For a form or an application: they usually ask for a maximum width (for example, 800 pixels) and a maximum file size. First adjust the size here and, if it's still too heavy, compress it.
  • For printing: use In centimeters; at 300 dpi, a 10 × 15 cm photo needs about 1181 × 1772 pixels to look sharp.

Your photos aren't uploaded

Everything happens in your browser, even with dozens of photos at once. Also, when saving, the photo's hidden data (EXIF) is removed, such as the GPS location where it was taken.

Quick summary

  1. Choose the photos. Go to docusmart.online/en/resize-image and choose one or several photos.
  2. Pick the size. By percentage, by pixels, a social media size or in centimeters.
  3. Resize. Press Resize and compare the before and after.
  4. Download. Download the photo, or the ZIP if there were several.

Frequently asked questions

Does a photo lose quality when you make it smaller?

Not to the eye: it looks just as sharp, only smaller. What you can't do is enlarge it without it showing.

Can I resize several photos at once?

Yes. Choose them all together: they're resized to the same size and downloaded in a ZIP.

What size should I use for Instagram?

In Social media you have Instagram square and portrait, and stories. The photo fills the size and whatever is left over is trimmed from the center.
