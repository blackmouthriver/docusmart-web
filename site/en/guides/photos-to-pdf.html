How to convert photos to PDF, several in one file

You're asked for "the document as a PDF" and all you have is photos in your gallery. Here is how to turn them into a tidy, readable and lightweight PDF without installing anything.

4 min readUpdated on November 18, 2026

Two options, depending on the photo

  • Photos that already look good (screenshots, downloaded images, photos taken straight on): use Image to PDF. Each photo becomes a page exactly as it is.
  • Photos of a sheet of paper (a receipt, a contract, a certificate): use Scan document. It finds the edges of the page, straightens it and whitens the paper so it looks scanned.

Step by step with Image to PDF

Open Image to PDF and choose your images. It accepts JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP and GIF, and you can mix them.

Put them in order with the arrows: each image will be one page, in that order. Tap "Convert" and you're done: you have a single PDF.

How to take a photo that's easy to read

  • Use even lighting and skip the direct flash: shadows from your hand or phone make the text harder to read.
  • Place the page on a dark surface (a wooden table, for example) so the scanner can find the edges more easily.
  • Hold the phone parallel to the page, not at an angle.
  • Make sure the whole page is in the frame, with a little margin around it.

In Scan document you can check "Make the PDF searchable": it recognizes the text so it can be searched and copied, which some online portals appreciate.

Keeping the file size down

A photo from a modern phone can weigh 3 or 4 MB, so ten photos already make a PDF that is heavy for an email. If the result is large, run it through Compress PDF at the "Medium" level, which greatly reduces pages that are photos. You can also compress the photos beforehand with Compress image.

If you have an iPhone

When you pick photos in Safari, the iPhone usually hands them over as JPG, so they work without any extra steps. If you copied the photos to your computer and they show up as .HEIC files, that format can't be converted here yet.

To make your iPhone save future photos as JPG, go to Settings › Camera › Formats and choose "Most Compatible". For photos you already have, send them to your computer from the Photos app using the share option: they usually arrive converted to JPG.

Your photos stay on your device

The conversion happens in your browser: the photos aren't uploaded to any server. That matters when they are ID documents or health information.

Frequently asked questions

Can I put several photos on one page?

Image to PDF puts one photo per page, which is what most forms and applications expect. If you need several on one sheet, combine them into a single image first.

How is this different from scanning?

Scan document crops the page, straightens it and improves the contrast; Image to PDF keeps the photo exactly as it is. For paper documents, scanning gives a cleaner result.

Does it work without internet?

Once the page is open, the conversion doesn't need a connection. If you install DocuSmart Desktop or the Android app, it works completely offline.
