How to black out personal data in a PDF (for real)

Before sharing a bank statement, a contract or a medical record, you often need to hide account or ID numbers. Putting a black box on top isn't enough: here is how to do it properly.

4 min readUpdated on November 25, 2026

Why a black box isn't enough

If you place a black rectangle over a number in an ordinary editor, the number is still in the file: anyone can select and copy it, or open the PDF in another program, and read it. Real redaction means deleting what is underneath, and that is what Redact PDF does.

Step by step

Open Redact PDF and choose the file. You can search for a name, a number or a word and it gets marked on every page at once. You can also mark all Emails or all Numbers (IDs, phone numbers, accounts) in one go, or drag over the page to cover an area by hand.

Check every page: tap an area to move it or remove it. When everything is ready, tap "Redact PDF" and download the result.

Pages with marked areas are turned into images so that what you covered disappears from the file. The rest of the text on those pages can still be searched and copied.

Check before you send it

The search finds what is written as text. If part of the document is a photo or a scan, any data in it won't show up in the search: mark it by hand by dragging over the page. And keep the original separately, because redacted content can't be recovered.

If the document is going to stay in other people's hands, you can also add a password with Protect PDF with a password or a watermark saying what you handed it over for.

What usually needs to be covered

  • ID numbers and bank account numbers.
  • Addresses, phone numbers and email addresses.
  • Signatures and photos of documents.
  • Health data, salaries or information about other people.

Without uploading the document

The whole process happens in your browser: the document with the uncovered data never leaves your device. That is exactly what you want when redacting: if you uploaded the original to an online service to cover it, the data would already be out of your hands.

Quick summary

  1. Open Redact PDF. Go to docusmart.online/en/redact-pdf and choose the file.
  2. Mark what needs hiding. Search for some text, mark all emails or numbers, or drag over the page.
  3. Check every page. Tap an area to move it or remove it.
  4. Redact and download. Tap Redact PDF: what you covered is deleted from the file.

Frequently asked questions

Can redacted content be recovered?

No. What you cover is deleted from the file; that is why it is a good idea to keep the original separately.

Does it work with scanned PDFs?

Yes, by marking the areas by hand. The automatic search only finds what is written as text.

Can the rest of the document still be searched?

Yes. Only the pages with marked areas are turned into images, and their remaining text is kept for searching and copying.
