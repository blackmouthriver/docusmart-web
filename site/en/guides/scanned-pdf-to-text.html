How to turn a scanned PDF into text you can copy and search

A scan is a photo of each page: you can read it, but you can't copy it or search for a word. OCR turns the text back into text, without retyping it.

4 min readUpdated on November 26, 2026

What OCR does

OCR stands for optical character recognition: the software looks at the image of each page and recognizes the letters. OCR: recognize text gives you two things at once:

  • The recognized text, to copy it or download it as .txt or Word, with the page each part comes from.
  • A searchable PDF: the same scan, identical to the eye, with an invisible layer of text on top. You can select, copy and search for words with Ctrl+F.

Step by step

Open OCR: recognize text and choose the scanned PDF (a JPG, PNG or WebP photo also works). Pick the Document language: recognition is far more accurate when it knows which language to expect. If the document mixes its language with English, tick that box too.

Leave Also create a searchable PDF ticked if you want the PDF, and press Recognize text. You will see the progress page by page. When it finishes, the PDF is ready to download and, below it, the text, which you can copy or download as .txt or Word.

The first time, the language model is downloaded (a few seconds) and stays saved in your browser, so next time it starts right away.

How to get a better result

OCR reads what it sees. A sharp, straight, well-lit scan is recognized almost without errors; a blurry, shadowy or very small photo isn't. If the result looks doubtful, DocuSmart warns you with the confidence percentage so you can check the text.

  • Rotated pages are detected and read the right way up automatically.
  • Pages that already have text (a mixed PDF) are left as they are, unless you tick Also recognize pages that already have text.
  • It works with printed text. Handwriting is rarely recognized well.

If what you want is to edit it in Word

If your goal is to correct the document and not just copy the text, go straight to PDF to Word with the OCR box ticked: it recognizes the scanned pages in the same step and builds an editable .docx. And if the searchable PDF is too heavy to send, run it through Compress PDF.

Your document stays on your device

Recognition runs in your browser with Tesseract, an open-source OCR engine. The PDF isn't uploaded to any server, so you can use it with ID cards, medical records or bank statements without leaving copies anywhere else.

Quick summary

  1. Open OCR. Go to docusmart.online/en/ocr-pdf and choose the scanned PDF or a photo.
  2. Pick the language. Choose the document's language and tick whether it also has English text.
  3. Recognize the text. Press Recognize text and wait for every page to finish.
  4. Download. Download the searchable PDF, or copy the text or download it as .txt or Word.

Frequently asked questions

What is a searchable PDF?

It's your same scan with an invisible layer of text on top: it looks the same, but you can select, copy and search for words.

How many pages can I recognize?

As many as you need: they are recognized one by one in your browser. A long document takes longer, but there is no page limit.

Does it recognize several languages?

Twelve: Spanish, English, Portuguese, French, Italian, German, Catalan, Basque, Russian, Japanese, Korean and Chinese. If the document mixes its language with English, tick that box.
