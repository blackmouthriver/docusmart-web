How to change an image format: HEIC, JPG, PNG or WebP

Each format is good for something different, and sometimes a website or a program only accepts one. Here you change the format of your photos in seconds, without installing anything.

4 min readUpdated on November 27, 2026

Which format suits you

  • JPG: the format for photos. It's small and opens on any device, website or form. It doesn't support transparent backgrounds.
  • PNG: no quality loss and with a transparent background. Ideal for logos, screenshots and drawings; with photos it's much larger.
  • WebP: the modern format of the web. It looks the same as a JPG at a much smaller size, as in the image: from 189 KB to 50 KB.
  • HEIC: the one iPhones use. It takes up little space, but many computers, websites and programs can't open it.

iPhone photos (HEIC) to JPG

Open HEIC to JPG and choose your HEIC photos, one or many. Adjust the JPG quality if you want (the preset value looks the same as the original) and press convert. If there were several, they're downloaded together in a ZIP.

The first time, the HEIC decoder (1.4 MB) is downloaded and saved: after that, conversions start right away.

If you'd rather your iPhone take photos directly in JPG, go to Settings → Camera → Formats and choose Most Compatible.

JPG to PNG, or any image to WebP

With JPG to PNG you convert a photo to PNG when a program requires it or when you're going to edit it several times without losing quality. With Image to WebP you reduce the size of the photos on a website or an online store without it showing.

Both work the same way: you choose one or several images, press the button and see the before and after with the size of each version, ready to download.

If you also need it smaller or a different size

Changing the format doesn't change the dimensions of the photo. If a form says it can't exceed a certain file size, use Compress image, which also accepts HEIC photos and delivers them as JPG; and if they ask for an exact width or height, Resize image.

Without uploading your photos

The conversion happens in your browser: your photos aren't uploaded to any server. When saving, hidden data (EXIF) is also removed, such as the location where they were taken.

Quick summary

  1. Choose the conversion. Open HEIC to JPG, JPG to PNG or Image to WebP on docusmart.online.
  2. Choose the photos. Add one or several images.
  3. Convert. Press the button and compare the before and after.
  4. Download. Download the image, or the ZIP if there were several.

Frequently asked questions

Why can't I open my iPhone photos on my computer?

Because they're in HEIC, a format that many computers and websites don't recognize. Convert them to JPG and they open anywhere.

Does a photo lose quality when converted to PNG?

No: PNG doesn't lose quality. But it doesn't recover any either; the photo stays the same as the original JPG and is larger.

Does WebP display in all browsers?

Yes, in all current browsers. Some old desktop programs still can't open it.
