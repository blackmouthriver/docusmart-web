How to edit a PDF without installing any software

Fixing a date, filling in a missing detail, highlighting a clause or crossing something out: you don't need paid software for any of that. Here is how to edit a PDF in your browser.

4 min readUpdated on November 25, 2026

The editor's tools

At the top of the page there is a toolbar with everything you can add in Edit PDF:

  • Text: tap the page where you want to write and type in the box below; choose the font, size, bold and color.
  • Replace: tap text that is already in the PDF and change it; the original is deleted from the document, not just covered up.
  • Rectangle, Circle, Highlight and Cover: to mark, underline or cover with white.
  • Draw, Line and Arrow: to point things out or make handwritten notes.
  • Image and Link: to add a stamp or a logo, or link to a website or to another page of the PDF.

Step by step

Open Edit PDF and choose the file. Pick "Text" on the toolbar and tap the page where it goes: a box appears that you can move, and you type in the panel below. With "Select" you tap any element to move it, resize it or remove it.

If you make a mistake, use "Undo". When you are done, tap "Apply changes" and download the edited PDF.

Text can be in any alphabet (Latin, Cyrillic, Greek, Chinese, Japanese or Korean). Emojis aren't saved in the PDF.

Signing and filling in forms have their own tools

If what you need is to sign, Sign PDF lets you draw, type or upload your signature and place it where it goes. And if the PDF has fields to fill in, Fill in PDF form shows them one by one, which is easier than typing on top.

Tips so the change doesn't show

To correct text that is already in the document, use Replace: it deletes the original and puts the new text in its place. If you only cover it with a white box, the old text is still in the file.

Pick the font closest to the document's (Sans, Serif or Mono) and adjust the size until it matches. On a phone, move elements carefully: you can also change their position from the keyboard with the arrow keys.

Your file stays with you

The whole editor runs inside your browser: the PDF isn't uploaded to any server, neither while you edit it nor when you save it.

Quick summary

  1. Open Edit PDF. Go to docusmart.online/en/edit-pdf and choose the file.
  2. Choose what to add. Text, replace, shapes, highlight, drawing, arrows, image or link.
  3. Place it on the page. Tap the page, type or draw, and move it with Select.
  4. Apply and download. Tap Apply changes and download the edited PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Can I change text that is already in the PDF?

Yes, with "Replace": tap the text, type the new one and the original is deleted from the document.

Does it work with scanned PDFs?

You can add text, shapes and drawings on top. To replace text, the PDF needs real text; if it is a photo, run it through OCR first.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It works in the browser on your computer or phone.
