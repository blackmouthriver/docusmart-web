How to scan documents with your phone

You don't need a scanner or an app: your phone's camera and browser are enough to get a clean, cropped PDF with the text recognized.

5 min readUpdated on November 18, 2026

What a scanner does (and why a photo alone isn't enough)

A photo of a page almost never comes out perfect: it's slightly tilted, the table shows around it and the white paper looks gray or shadowy. A phone scanner fixes that: it finds the four corners of the page, straightens it as if you had photographed it from directly above and whitens the background.

That's what Scan document does, right in your browser.

Step by step

Open Scan document and tap "Take photo": on your phone, the camera opens. You can also choose photos you already have.

Check the corners DocuSmart found; if one is off, drag it to the tip of the page. Choose the "Document" filter to whiten the paper and remove shadows. Add more pages if the document has several sheets.

Tap "Create PDF". If you check "Make the PDF searchable", the text is also recognized so you can search for words and copy them.

Five tips for a sharp scan

  • Put the page on a dark surface: the contrast helps detect the edges.
  • Use natural light or a lamp from the side; avoid the direct flash, which causes glare.
  • Hold the phone parallel to the page and wait for it to focus.
  • Flatten the page well: folds create shadows the filter can't always remove.
  • For multiple pages, scan them all in the same session and they'll come out as a single PDF.

Recognizing text in a scan you already have

If you already have a scanned PDF or a photo and need to copy its text, use OCR: it recognizes printed text in 12 languages (Spanish, English, Portuguese, French, Russian, Japanese, Chinese and more) and gives it to you to copy, as a .txt file or as a searchable PDF. It works best with sharp, straight scans; handwriting is usually recognized poorly.

Private from start to finish

Edge detection, straightening, filters and text recognition all happen inside your browser. The photos aren't uploaded to any server. If you scan often, the DocuSmart app for Android does the same thing offline.

Quick summary

  1. Open Scan document. Go to the Scan document tool on docusmart.online and tap "Take photo".
  2. Adjust the corners. Check the crop and drag the corners if needed.
  3. Choose the filter. The "Document" filter whitens the paper and removes shadows.
  4. Save the PDF. Tap "Create PDF"; check "Make the PDF searchable" to recognize the text.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install an app?

No. It works in the browser on your phone or computer. The Android app is optional, for scanning offline.

What is a searchable PDF?

A PDF that, in addition to the image, contains the recognized text: you can search for words, select them and copy them.

What if it doesn't find the edges properly?

Drag each corner to the tip of the page or use "Whole photo". It helps if the paper contrasts with the background.
