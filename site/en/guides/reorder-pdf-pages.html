How to change the order of the pages in a PDF

A double-sided scan that came out interleaved, an appendix that should have gone first, or a page that appears twice: here is how to put a PDF in order, page by page.

3 min readUpdated on November 25, 2026

What you can do with each page

In Reorder PDF pages each page is a card, and with it you can:

  • Move it: drag it into place, or use the earlier and later arrows (easier on a phone).
  • Duplicate it: for example, to repeat a cover page or a signature page.
  • Add a blank page after it, to leave space or separate sections.
  • Remove it if you don't need it.

Step by step

Open Reorder PDF pages and choose the PDF. Move the pages until they are the way you need them: the number on each card ("P. 2", "P. 1"…) reminds you where it came from, so you don't lose track.

When the order is right, tap "Apply new order" and download the PDF. The pages are copied as they are: neither the text nor the quality changes.

If the pages you want to reorder are in several files, merge them first with Merge PDF and then reorder them here.

To take pages out, splitting is better

If you only want to keep some of the pages, Split PDF is quicker: you mark the ones you want and you're done. Reordering is for when the whole document stays, just in a different order.

Common cases

When you scan double-sided on a machine that can't do it automatically, you get all the fronts first and then all the backs. To interleave them, move the last page after the first one, the second-to-last after the third, and so on to the end: with the arrows it goes quickly.

  • An appendix that should have gone first: move it before page one.
  • A signature page that belongs in two places: duplicate it and move the copy where it's needed.
  • Chapters that must start on an odd page when printing double-sided: add a blank page before them.

No need to upload your files

The new order is put together in your browser. The document doesn't travel over the internet and no copy stays on any server. That is why you can safely reorder case files, medical records or work documents that shouldn't leave your device.

Quick summary

  1. Open Reorder PDF pages. Go to docusmart.online/en/reorder-pdf-pages and choose the PDF.
  2. Move the pages. Drag them or use the arrows; duplicate pages, add blank ones or remove the ones you don't need.
  3. Apply and download. Tap Apply new order and download the reordered PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Can I reorder pages on my phone?

Yes. Besides dragging, each page has arrows to move it earlier or later, which are easier to use with your finger.

Are links and forms kept?

The pages are copied with their content; check the result if the document has complex forms.

Is there a page limit?

There is no fixed number; with very large documents the thumbnails may take a little longer to appear.
