How to convert a PDF to JPG or get out the photos inside it

To post a page on social media, send it on WhatsApp as an image or recover a photo you only have inside a PDF. These are two different tools, and it helps to know which one to use.

4 min readUpdated on November 26, 2026

Whole page or just the photos?

  • PDF to JPG turns each whole page into an image, with its text, its photos and its layout, like a screenshot but in good quality.
  • Extract images from a PDF gets out only the photos and graphics inside it, each one separately and at its original size, without the text around them.

If you want to show a page exactly as it looks, use PDF to JPG. If you want the logo, the product photo or the floor plan that comes inside the document, use Extract images.

PDF to JPG, step by step

Open PDF to JPG and choose the PDF. Pick the Image format and the Resolution:

  • PNG keeps the exact quality and is better for pages with text; JPG is lighter and ideal for sharing. With JPG you also choose the quality.
  • 72 dpi for viewing on screen, 150 dpi for almost everything and 300 dpi for printing.

If you only want some pages, type them in Pages (for example, 1-3, 5); leaving it empty converts them all. Press Convert: you will see each image with its page number, and if there are several they are downloaded together in a ZIP.

Extract images, step by step

Open Extract images from a PDF, choose the PDF and press Find images. All the photos and graphics appear with their page, their size and their format. Choose the ones you want and download them one by one or all in a ZIP.

JPEG photos come out identical to the originals, byte for byte; the rest are saved as PNG, with no loss. Leave Skip small images ticked so you don't end up with a pile of icons and bullets.

In a scanned PDF each page is a single large image, so Extract images gives you back the pages. In that case either tool will do.

If the image is too heavy

At 300 dpi an A4 page is more than 2,400 pixels wide. If you need it lighter for the web or a form, run it through Compress image. And everything happens in your browser: the PDF isn't uploaded to any server.

Quick summary

  1. Choose the tool. PDF to JPG for whole pages; Extract images for the photos inside it.
  2. Choose the PDF. Open the tool on docusmart.online and choose the file.
  3. Adjust and convert. In PDF to JPG choose format, resolution and pages; in Extract images press Find images and choose the ones you want.
  4. Download. Download each image or all of them together in a ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

Which resolution should I choose?

150 dpi works for almost everything. 72 dpi if it will only be viewed on screen and 300 dpi if it will be printed.

Do extracted photos lose quality?

No. JPEG photos are copied identical to the originals and the rest are saved as PNG, with no loss.

Can I convert just one page?

Yes. In PDF to JPG type its number in Pages, for example 3, and only that one is converted.
