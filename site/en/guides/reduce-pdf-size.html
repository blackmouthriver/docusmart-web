How to reduce the size of a PDF

"The file exceeds the maximum allowed size." If you got that message on a portal or in your email, this guide explains why it happens and how to fix it without making the document unreadable.

5 min readUpdated on November 18, 2026

Why a PDF is so large

Images are what take up the most space in a PDF. A text-only document created in Word is usually a few hundred kilobytes, even with many pages. One put together from photos, or from a scanner set to high resolution, can easily go over 10 MB with just a few pages.

That's why compression works very differently depending on the type of PDF: a text document has little to reduce, while a scan has a lot.

Which level to choose

  • Lossless: optimizes the structure of the file without touching the quality. The text stays selectable. It's the safe choice for contracts, invoices and digital documents.
  • Medium: recommended for scans and photos. It cuts the size a lot and stays easy to read.
  • High: for when the limit is very strict. Keep in mind that at the Medium and High levels the text becomes an image, so it can no longer be selected.

Start with "Lossless". If that doesn't get you to the size you need, try "Medium". DocuSmart shows you how much it shrank before you send it.

Step by step

Open Compress PDF, choose the file and pick the level. Tap "Compress PDF": you'll see the size before and after, and the result downloads.

If the PDF was already optimized and can't be reduced any further, DocuSmart tells you and doesn't download a copy that is the same size or larger.

If it's still too large

  • Split the document: many portals accept several attachments. With Split PDF you can break it into two or more files.
  • Compress the photos before making the PDF: Compress image shrinks a phone photo by 60% to 90% with almost no visible difference.
  • Remove pages you don't need: with Reorder PDF pages you can delete cover pages or blank pages.

Common limits

For reference: Gmail allows attachments up to 25 MB and Outlook up to 20 MB. Many government and banking portals ask for files between 2 and 5 MB each. Always check the limit of the site you're uploading to.

Quick summary

  1. Open Compress PDF. Go to the Compress PDF tool on docusmart.online.
  2. Choose the file. Select the PDF that is too large.
  3. Pick the level. "Lossless" for digital documents; "Medium" or "High" for scans and photos.
  4. Compress. Tap "Compress PDF", check how much it shrank and download the result.

Frequently asked questions

Does compressing damage the document?

With "Lossless", no: the content stays identical. With "Medium" and "High" the pages are turned into lighter images, which read well but lose selectable text.

Is my PDF uploaded to the internet?

No. Compression happens inside your browser: the file never leaves your device.

Why did my PDF barely shrink?

It's most likely a text PDF or one that was already optimized. In those cases the size is already small and there is little to remove.
