How to make an invoice for your services in PDF

A bill for services states who owes whom, how much and for what. With the DocuSmart template you fill it in your browser and get a tidy PDF, ready to send.

5 min readUpdated on November 27, 2026

What a bill for services is and who uses it

It's the document a person uses to charge for a job or a service when they don't issue a formal invoice: a freelance designer, a private tutor, a contractor who provides services to a company or someone who rents out a property. It must state who is charging, whom, the date, the description, the amount (in figures and in words) and how it's paid.

A bill for services doesn't replace an electronic invoice. If your activity or your income requires you to issue invoices, check with an accountant or with your country's tax authority.

Step by step

Open Invoice, quote and receipt maker and keep the Bill for services type selected. Search for your Country: this sets the currency, the name of the tax and the types of ID (national ID, tax IDs such as NIT, RFC or RUC…). The document number is suggested for you (CC-0001) and the date is today's; add the city and, if you want, a Payment due date.

In Your details enter your name and your ID number; in Client, those of the person paying you. The address, phone and email are optional.

In Items describe what you're charging for, the quantity and the unit price. If there are several things, use Add item. The subtotal, tax, withholding and total are calculated as you type, and the amount in words appears below.

Tax, withholding and discount

If you charge a tax (such as VAT), enter its percentage; when you choose your country, a button appears to use the standard rate. If the client applies withholding, enter the percentage and it's subtracted from the total, as in the image: a subtotal of 1,800,000, 10% withholding and 1,620,000 to pay. The discount is subtracted before taxes.

The withholding percentage depends on your country, the type of service and your tax situation. If you don't know it, ask the client: they almost always tell you how much they'll withhold.

Payment details, signature and PDF

In Payment details enter the bank, the account type and number and the account holder's name, so the client knows where to pay. In Notes you can write deadlines or terms. With Add signature you draw it with your finger or the mouse, and with Add logo you put yours at the top.

Press Create PDF: you'll see the finished bill, with the amount in words and the signature line, ready to download, share on WhatsApp or save to Drive.

  • The number continues on its own with each new document (CC-0002, CC-0003…). And if you check Remember my details, logo, clients, items and history in this browser, next time your details, logo and clients are already filled in; the bank account is never saved.
  • In Issued documents you can open an earlier bill to correct it, or turn an approved quote into a bill for services without typing everything again.
  • With the same tool you can make a quote (with an expiry date) or a payment receipt.

Your data and your client's aren't uploaded

Everything is put together in your browser: names, ID numbers and amounts aren't sent to any server. If the client asks for the bill signed on a PDF you already have, use Sign PDF; and if you're sending several together by email, Compress PDF makes them lighter.

Quick summary

  1. Open the template. Go to docusmart.online/en/invoice-generator and choose your country.
  2. Enter the details. Fill in your details, the client's, the city and the date.
  3. Add what you charge. Describe each item with its amount; the total, the withholding and the amount in words are calculated for you.
  4. Create the PDF. Add the payment details and your signature, press Create PDF and download it.

Frequently asked questions

Is a bill for services the same as a formal invoice?

No. A bill for services is used by people who aren't required to issue invoices; it doesn't replace an electronic invoice. DocuSmart doesn't issue electronic invoices.

How is withholding calculated?

Enter the percentage your client gives you and DocuSmart subtracts it from the subtotal. The total to pay already has the withholding deducted.

Can I make a bill in another currency?

Yes. Choosing your country sets its currency, and you can change it to another one (dollars, euros…) in the Currency field.
