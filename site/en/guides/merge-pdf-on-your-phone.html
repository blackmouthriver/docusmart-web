How to merge PDFs on your phone without uploading them

Putting your ID card, a receipt and an application form into a single PDF is one of the most common steps in any online application. Here is how to do it from your phone in about a minute, without handing your documents to someone else's server.

4 min readUpdated on November 18, 2026

Why it matters that your files aren't uploaded

Most websites that merge PDFs receive your documents on their servers, process them there and send the result back. For a restaurant menu that hardly matters. For an ID card, a bank statement or a medical record it does: you don't know how long they keep the files or who can see them.

DocuSmart merges the files inside your phone's browser. The file never travels over the internet, so no copy ends up on any server. That is also why it works with a weak signal once the page has loaded.

Before you start

  • Have the PDFs saved on your phone (in Downloads, in the Files app or in your cloud storage).
  • If one of them is a photo rather than a PDF, convert it first with Image to PDF.
  • If a PDF has a password, remove it first with Unlock PDF (you need to know the password).

Step by step

Open Merge PDF in your phone's browser (Chrome, Safari, Samsung Internet or whichever you use). Tap the box to choose your files: you can select several at once.

Check the order of the list. The first file will be the start of the final document; move the files with the arrows until they are in the order you need. Then tap "Merge": the combined PDF downloads with all the pages.

Need to rearrange individual pages rather than whole files? Merge first, then open the result in Reorder PDF pages, where you can move or remove each page.

If the final PDF is too large

Many government portals, application forms and email services limit attachment size (2 MB, 5 MB and 10 MB are common limits). If your file is over the limit, use Compress PDF: the "Lossless" level doesn't touch the quality, and "Medium" shrinks PDFs made from scans or photos much further.

If you do this often

The website works on any phone. If you use Android and merge documents regularly, the DocuSmart app does the same thing offline and keeps your files organized on your phone.

Quick summary

  1. Open Merge PDF. Go to the Merge PDF tool on docusmart.online in your phone's browser.
  2. Choose the PDFs. Tap the box and select all the files you want to combine.
  3. Put them in order. Move the files with the arrows: the first one will be the start of the document.
  4. Merge and download. Tap "Merge" and a single PDF with all the pages is downloaded.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes. It works in Safari and in any modern browser, on iPhone and on Android. The offline app is only available for Android.

How many PDFs can I merge?

There is no fixed number. The limit is your phone's memory: very large documents may take a little longer.

Is any quality lost?

No. The pages are copied as they are, with their original text, images and size.
