How to sign a PDF without printing it

Print, sign with a pen, scan and send it back: four steps you can replace with one. Here is how to sign a PDF from your phone or computer.

4 min readUpdated on November 18, 2026

Three ways to create your signature

  • Draw it with your finger on your phone or with the mouse on your computer.
  • Type your name, which is shown in a handwriting-style font.
  • Upload a photo of your signature on paper. This is the one that looks most like your usual signature.

For the photo: sign in dark ink on a white sheet, in good light and without shadows. DocuSmart removes the background so only the stroke remains.

Step by step

Open Sign PDF and choose the document. Create your signature using any of the three methods.

Drag it to where the signature goes and adjust its size. If the document needs initials on every page, use "Repeat on every page". Tap "Sign PDF".

If you also need to fill in details

Many documents that need a signature are forms: name, ID number, date. If the PDF has fillable fields, complete them first with Fill in PDF form and then sign it. Before sending it, check "Flatten" so the answers are locked in place.

Common signing mistakes

  • A tiny or pixelated signature: enlarge it until it fills the signature line; if it comes from a photo, take the photo up close and in good light.
  • A signature on top of the text: move it so it doesn't cover any details in the document; a contract with the signature over a clause can raise questions.
  • Signing the wrong version: make sure it's the final document before you sign; fixing it afterwards means signing again.
  • Forgetting the other pages: if the document needs a signature or initials on every page, use "Repeat on every page" instead of going one by one.

Is it legally valid?

What you get is a simple electronic signature: an image of your signature on the document, the digital equivalent of signing by hand. It works for most everyday paperwork: rental agreements, authorizations, company forms or school permission slips.

Some procedures require a certificate-based digital signature, issued by an authorized certification provider; for example, certain filings with government agencies or electronic invoicing. If the procedure explicitly asks for one, an image signature isn't enough.

Your signature isn't stored anywhere

The signature and the document are processed inside your browser. They aren't uploaded to a server or saved: when you close the page, the signature is gone. If you're going to send the document through a channel you don't fully trust, you can add a password with Protect PDF with a password.

Quick summary

  1. Open Sign PDF. Go to the Sign PDF tool on docusmart.online and choose the document.
  2. Create your signature. Draw it, type your name or upload a photo of your signature on paper.
  3. Place it. Drag it into place and adjust the size; you can repeat it on every page.
  4. Download. Tap "Sign PDF" to get the signed PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Can I sign from my phone?

Yes. On your phone you can draw the signature with your finger or take a photo of your signature on paper.

Can the signed document be modified?

The signature is embedded in the page. To prevent changes and unwanted access, you can protect the PDF with a password and turn off the permission to modify it.

Can I sign several pages?

Yes: add the signature to each page you want or use "Repeat on every page".
