How to repair a PDF that won't open, shows blank or gives an error

"The file is damaged and could not be repaired", a blank screen or a download that got cut off. Before you give the document up for lost, try rebuilding it.

3 min readUpdated on November 26, 2026

Why a PDF gets damaged

At the end of the file, a PDF has an index that says where each page and each image begins. If that index is lost or shifted, the reader can't find anything and shows an error, even though the content is still there. It happens mostly with:

  • Downloads or attachments that were cut off halfway.
  • Files copied to a USB drive that was removed too early.
  • Programs that saved the PDF badly or only half-edited it.

The signs are always similar: the reader says the file is damaged, it opens but the pages are blank, or it looks fine on your device and a portal rejects it when you upload it.

Step by step

Open Repair PDF, choose the damaged file and press Repair PDF. The tool reads the file from start to finish, finds the pages even if the index is broken and writes a new, healthy copy.

When it finishes you will see the repaired copy next to the original and it will tell you what happened: whether it found and fixed problems, whether the file was fine, or, if it was cut off, how many pages were rescued out of how many it had.

Even if the PDF opens, repairing it is sometimes worth it: if a portal rejects it or a tool gives an error, a rewritten copy usually solves it.

What can and can't be recovered

Whatever arrived complete is recovered. If a download was cut off at 70%, the pages within that 70% are rescued; the ones at the end, which never arrived, can't be rebuilt. In that case the best thing is to download the file again or ask for it again, and use the repaired copy in the meantime.

If the PDF needs a password to open, remove it first with Unlock PDF and then repair it. And if what you rescued are scanned pages and you need their text, use OCR: recognize text.

Your document stays on your device

The repair happens in your browser: the file isn't uploaded to any server. You can try it with personal or work documents without leaving copies anywhere else, and the original isn't modified.

Quick summary

  1. Open Repair PDF. Go to docusmart.online/en/repair-pdf and choose the damaged PDF.
  2. Repair. Press Repair PDF: its structure is rebuilt.
  3. Check the result. See what was fixed and how many pages were rescued.
  4. Download. Download the repaired copy; the original doesn't change.

Frequently asked questions

Can it recover a file that was cut off?

Yes, the pages that managed to arrive complete. The ones missing from the end of the file can't be rebuilt.

Is my original file modified?

No. A separate repaired copy is downloaded.

Does it work with password-protected PDFs?

First remove the password with Unlock PDF and then repair it.
