How to rotate PDF pages (and keep them that way when you save)

It happens a lot with scans: a landscape sheet ends up sideways, or a page comes out upside down. Rotating it in the viewer doesn't help, because it looks wrong again once you send it. Here is how to fix it in the file itself.

3 min readUpdated on November 25, 2026

Why rotating it in the viewer isn't enough

PDF viewers let you rotate the view, but that rotation isn't saved in the file: whoever receives it will see it crooked again. To fix it for good, you have to change the page orientation inside the PDF, which is what Rotate PDF does.

Step by step

Open Rotate PDF and choose the file. Each page appears as a thumbnail with its current rotation underneath (0°, 90°…). Tap a page to rotate it 90°, or use the arrows on each one to turn it left or right. If the whole document is sideways, use "All to the left" or "All to the right".

Once every page looks right, tap "Rotate PDF" and download the result. Only the orientation changes: the text, the images and the quality stay the same.

If the pages are also out of order (common when scanning both sides), continue with Reorder PDF pages.

Scans that come out crooked

Rotating works for pages that are sideways or upside down, in 90° steps. If the photo is tilted by a few degrees, it is better to scan it again with Scan document, which detects the edges of the sheet and straightens it automatically.

Common cases

When scanning double-sided, the back of each sheet often comes out upside down: tap those pages twice to rotate them 180°. In a report with landscape tables, rotate only those pages so they can be read without tilting your head; the rest stay as they are.

If the PDF comes from phone photos, check the orientation when you create it: Image to PDF has automatic, portrait or landscape orientation, so you won't have to rotate anything afterwards.

Private from start to finish

The PDF is rotated inside your browser. It isn't uploaded to any server and isn't stored anywhere: when you close the tab, it's gone. It works the same on a computer and on a phone, and once the page has loaded it doesn't need a good signal.

Quick summary

  1. Open Rotate PDF. Go to docusmart.online/en/rotate-pdf and choose the PDF.
  2. Rotate the pages. Tap each page that is sideways, or use All to the left or All to the right.
  3. Save. Tap Rotate PDF and download the file, now the right way up.

Frequently asked questions

Can I rotate just one page?

Yes. Each page is rotated separately; the others stay as they were.

Do I lose quality?

No. Only the page orientation changes; the content isn't compressed again.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, in any modern browser, on Android and iPhone.
