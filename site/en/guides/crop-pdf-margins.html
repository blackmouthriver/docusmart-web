How to crop the margins or part of a PDF

A PDF with huge margins reads small on your phone and prints with lots of blank paper. Cropping it leaves only what matters, without touching the content.

3 min readUpdated on November 27, 2026

Step by step

Open Crop PDF and choose the file. You'll see the first page with a box on top of it: the darkened area is what will be cropped.

Press Remove blank margins and the box automatically fits the content of the page, as in the image. If you'd rather do it by hand, drag the edges or corners of the box. Below it you see the size that will remain, in millimeters.

Choose whether the crop applies to All pages or only to the one you're viewing (Only page 1, for example), press Crop PDF and download the result.

If the pages have different margins, check several with the arrows before cropping: the box for “All pages” is the same for each one.

What it's for

  • Reading on your phone: without margins, the text fills the whole screen and reads larger.
  • Pulling out a part: a table, a chart or the photo in a scanned document, to use it separately.
  • Removing leftovers from a scan: the black edge of the scanner lid or the table around the page.

What happens to the content

Cropping changes the visible area of each page, without redoing the text or the images: what remains looks just as sharp and the text can still be copied and searched.

If you then need only some pages, separate them with Split PDF; and if one ended up sideways, straighten it with Rotate PDF.

Before printing

When you print a cropped PDF, the page comes out smaller than the sheet. To make it fill the whole paper, choose Fit to page in the print dialog of your browser or PDF viewer.

If you cropped a scan to keep the photo of a document (an ID card, a badge), check that you didn't cut off any edge of the document: many applications require all four corners to be visible.

Your PDF doesn't leave your device

All the cropping happens in your browser. The PDF isn't uploaded to any server, so you can use it with contracts or personal documents with peace of mind.

Quick summary

  1. Open Crop PDF. Go to docusmart.online/en/crop-pdf and choose the PDF.
  2. Adjust the box. Press Remove blank margins or drag the edges by hand.
  3. Choose the pages. Apply the crop to all pages or only to the one you're viewing.
  4. Download. Press Crop PDF and download the result.

Tools in this guide

  • Crop PDFRemove margins or keep an area
  • Split PDFExtract or separate pages
  • Rotate PDFStraighten rotated pages

Frequently asked questions

Does cropping a PDF reduce its quality?

No. Only the visible area of the page changes; the text and images stay the same.

Can I crop just one page?

Yes. Before cropping, choose the Only page option for the page you're viewing.

Is the cropped part deleted from the file?

It's left out of view and out of printing. If what you want is to delete private data, use Redact PDF, which really removes it.
