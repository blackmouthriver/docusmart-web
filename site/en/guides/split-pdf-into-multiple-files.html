How to split a PDF into several files or extract just a few pages

A portal only wants the signature page, or an 80-page PDF is too big to email. Here is how to break a PDF into parts, or keep only the pages you need, right from your browser.

4 min readUpdated on November 25, 2026

Six ways to split

Before you start, it helps to know what you need, because Split PDF has a mode for each case:

  • Choose pages: tap the thumbnails (or type "1-3, 5") and you get a PDF with only those pages.
  • By ranges: several ranges at once, and each one becomes a separate PDF.
  • Every N pages: equal parts, for example 10 pages each.
  • By bookmarks: one PDF per chapter, if the document has a bookmark outline.
  • By size: parts that stay under a given size, for email or portals with a limit.
  • Each page separately: one PDF per page.

Step by step

Open Split PDF and choose the file. You will see its pages as thumbnails. In "Choose pages", tap the ones you want to keep: they turn blue and the field above fills in by itself.

Tap the button at the bottom. When it finishes, you will see the result next to the original so you can check it, and you can download it with any name you like. If the mode creates several files, you can get them one by one or all together in a ZIP.

To take a few pages from several PDFs and put them into one, Merge PDF is quicker: there you can pick which pages to take from each file.

If the parts are still too large

Splitting doesn't change the quality: each page is copied as it is. If a part is still over the limit, use the By size mode or run it through Compress PDF. A single page scanned at a high resolution can be bigger than the limit on its own: in that case, compressing it is what helps.

Common cases

  • A portal only wants both sides of your ID card: choose those two pages and upload just that PDF.
  • A statement covering the whole year: if it has bookmarks for each month, use By bookmarks; if not, Every N pages.
  • An email with a 10 MB limit: use By size, enter 10 and every part stays below it.

Your document stays on your device

The split happens in your browser: the PDF isn't uploaded to any server. That is why you can use it with bank statements, medical records or contracts without leaving copies anywhere else.

Quick summary

  1. Open Split PDF. Go to docusmart.online/en/split-pdf and choose the file.
  2. Choose the mode. Choose pages, by ranges, every N pages, by bookmarks, by size or each page separately.
  3. Mark the pages. Tap the thumbnails or type the numbers (for example, 1-3, 5).
  4. Split and download. Tap the button and download the result, or all the files in a ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

Do I lose quality when splitting?

No. The pages are copied as they are, with their original text and images.

Can I split a password-protected PDF?

If it only has restrictions (it opens without a password), yes. If it asks for a password to open, remove it first with Unlock PDF.

What happens to the bookmarks?

In By bookmarks mode, each chapter in the outline becomes a PDF named after its bookmark.
