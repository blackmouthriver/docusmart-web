Frequently asked questions

What people usually ask us.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. DocuSmart converts, scans and edits your documents on the phone itself. Your files stay on your device.

Do I need internet to use it?

Not to open, convert and edit documents. A connection is only used for things like buying or restoring Premium through Google Play.

What does the Premium trial include?

When you install the app you get 3 days with all Premium features, without being asked for a card. When it ends, you continue with the free version unless you decide to subscribe.

Which formats can I convert?

Images to PDF, JPG, PNG, WebP and BMP; PDF to image, TXT, Word and HTML; Word to PDF, TXT and HTML; Excel to PDF, CSV and HTML; and PowerPoint to PDF and TXT. 17 combinations in total.

How do I cancel the subscription?

From Google Play, under Payments & subscriptions, or from Settings → Premium inside the app, with the "Manage subscription" button.
