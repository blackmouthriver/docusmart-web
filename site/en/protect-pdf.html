Protect PDF with a password

Encrypt your PDF so only someone with the password can open it, and control what can be done with it.

  • No file uploads
  • Free, no sign-up
  • Works in your browser

How to password-protect a PDF in 3 steps

  1. Choose the PDF you want to protect.
  2. Type the password twice and choose whether to allow printing, copying or modifying.
  3. Press "Protect PDF" and the encrypted document is downloaded.

Your files never leave your device

Unlike other online tools, DocuSmart doesn't upload your documents to any server: everything is processed inside your browser. You can use it with contracts, invoices or ID documents with complete peace of mind. Read our privacy policy (in Spanish).

Frequently asked questions

How secure is it?

It uses AES-256 encryption, the strongest PDF standard. It opens in Adobe Acrobat, Chrome, Edge and the DocuSmart app.

What if I forget the password?

It can't be recovered: not even we can open the PDF, because the password never leaves your device. Keep it somewhere safe.

Is my document uploaded to a server?

No. Encryption happens in your browser; neither the file nor the password is sent over the internet.
