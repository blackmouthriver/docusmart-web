Strong password generator

Create a strong, random password for every account in one click, with your own browser's cryptographic generator: nobody else sees it.

  • No file uploads
  • Free, no sign-up
  • Works in your browser

How to create a strong password in 3 steps

  1. Choose the length and which characters to use: lowercase, uppercase, numbers and symbols.
  2. Check the estimated strength; if you want a different one, press "Generate another".
  3. Press "Copy" and save it in your password manager.

Your files never leave your device

Unlike other online tools, DocuSmart doesn't upload your documents to any server: everything is processed inside your browser. You can use it with contracts, invoices or ID documents with complete peace of mind. Read our privacy policy (in Spanish).

Frequently asked questions

How secure is it?

It uses the browser's cryptographic generator (the same one that protects secure connections) and doesn't repeat patterns. With 16 characters of every type, brute-forcing it would take billions of years.

Is my password saved or sent?

No. It's generated on your device and disappears when you close the page. DocuSmart never sees or stores it.

What length should I use?

At least 12 characters; 16 or more for important accounts like email or banking. Each extra character makes it much harder to guess.

Why avoid look-alike characters?

It removes letters and numbers that are easily confused, such as I, l, 1, O and 0, in case you need to type the password by hand or read it out.

Can I use the same password on several sites?

Better not: if one site is breached, all your accounts would be exposed. Generate a different one for each site and keep them in a password manager.
