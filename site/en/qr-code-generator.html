Create and read QR codes

Generate a QR for a link or a text, protect it with a password if you like, or read one with the camera.

  • No file uploads
  • Free, no sign-up
  • Works in your browser

How to create a QR code in 3 steps

  1. Type the link or text and press "Generate QR".
  2. If it's private, choose "Protected" and set a password: the content is encrypted.
  3. Download the PNG, or use "Read QR" with the camera or an image.

Your files never leave your device

Unlike other online tools, DocuSmart doesn't upload your documents to any server: everything is processed inside your browser. You can use it with contracts, invoices or ID documents with complete peace of mind. Read our privacy policy (in Spanish).

Frequently asked questions

What is a protected QR?

A QR whose content is encrypted with AES-256: whoever scans it needs the password to see it.

Can I open it in the DocuSmart app?

Yes. Protected QR codes from the web and the app use the same format, so they work in both.

Is what I scan stored?

No. The camera image is analysed in your browser and isn't recorded or sent anywhere.
