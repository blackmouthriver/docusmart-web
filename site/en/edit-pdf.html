Edit PDF

Fill in a form that isn't editable, fix a detail, mark what matters or add your logo, right on the PDF's pages.

  • No file uploads
  • Free, no sign-up
  • Works in your browser

How to edit a PDF in 3 steps

  1. Choose the PDF.
  2. Use the toolbar: Text, Image, Rectangle, Circle, Highlight or Cover. Tap or drag on the page to add them.
  3. Adjust size, colour and position, and press "Apply changes".

Your files never leave your device

Unlike other online tools, DocuSmart doesn't upload your documents to any server: everything is processed inside your browser. You can use it with contracts, invoices or ID documents with complete peace of mind. Read our privacy policy (in Spanish).

Frequently asked questions

Can I change the text already in the PDF?

You can cover it with the "Cover" tool (a white box) and write the new text on top with "Text". The result looks as if you had corrected it.

Does "Cover" delete what's underneath?

No: it covers it, but the original text is still inside the file. If you need to truly remove sensitive data, use "Redact PDF".

Which fonts can I use?

Three standard PDF families (sans, serif and monospace), in regular or bold, with accented letters. Emojis and other alphabets cannot be saved with those fonts.

Are my files uploaded to the internet?

No. Everything happens inside your browser, without signing up.
