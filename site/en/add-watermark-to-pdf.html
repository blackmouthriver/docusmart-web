Watermark PDF

Type a text and it appears diagonally and semi-transparent on every page, without hiding the content. Ideal for marking copies or drafts.

  • No file uploads
  • Free, no sign-up
  • Works in your browser

How to add a watermark to a PDF in 3 steps

  1. Choose or drag in the PDF.
  2. Type the watermark text (up to 40 characters), for example "CONFIDENTIAL".
  3. Press "Apply watermark" and download the PDF.

Your files never leave your device

Unlike other online tools, DocuSmart doesn't upload your documents to any server: everything is processed inside your browser. You can use it with contracts, invoices or ID documents with complete peace of mind. Read our privacy policy (in Spanish).

Frequently asked questions

What does the watermark look like?

Large diagonal text in semi-transparent blue, in the center of each page. The content can still be read underneath.

Can I use accents?

Yes. The watermark supports Latin-alphabet letters with accents, such as é, ñ or ü. Other alphabets (Cyrillic, Chinese, Japanese, Korean) are not supported yet.

Can it be removed later?

The watermark is built into the pages of the new PDF. Keep your original in case you need a version without it later.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. The watermark is added inside your browser; the document never leaves your device.
