Compress image

Make your photos lighter to send them by email or WhatsApp, upload them to a website or save space, with no visible quality loss.

  • No file uploads
  • Free, no sign-up
  • Works in your browser

How to compress images in 3 steps

  1. Choose one or more images (JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF or BMP).
  2. Pick the level: low, recommended or maximum.
  3. Press "Compress" and download each image or all of them in a ZIP; you'll see how much space you saved.

Your files never leave your device

Unlike other online tools, DocuSmart doesn't upload your documents to any server: everything is processed inside your browser. You can use it with contracts, invoices or ID documents with complete peace of mind. Read our privacy policy (in Spanish).

Frequently asked questions

How much smaller do they get?

It depends on the photo: with the recommended level, a phone photo usually shrinks by 60% to 90%. If an image was already optimised and can't be reduced, DocuSmart gives you back the original.

Is the difference noticeable?

With the low and recommended levels, hardly at a glance. The maximum level also scales the image down to 1600 px per side, ideal for messaging and email.

What about images with a transparent background?

They're saved as WebP, which keeps the transparency and is much smaller than PNG.

Are my photos uploaded to the internet?

No. Compression happens inside your browser. The photo's hidden data (EXIF), such as GPS location, is also removed.
