Audio to text (transcription)

Turn a WhatsApp voice note, a meeting, an interview or a video's audio into text, right in your browser and without the audio leaving your device.

  • No file uploads
  • Free, no sign-up
  • Works in your browser

How to turn audio into text in 3 steps

  1. Choose the audio or video, or press "Record with the microphone" to dictate.
  2. Pick the language (or let it be detected) and press "Transcribe".
  3. Review the text and copy it or download it as .txt or Word, or the subtitles as .srt and .vtt.

Your files never leave your device

Unlike other online tools, DocuSmart doesn't upload your documents to any server: everything is processed inside your browser. You can use it with contracts, invoices or ID documents with complete peace of mind. Read our privacy policy (in Spanish).

Frequently asked questions

How does it work without uploading the audio?

It uses Whisper, an open-source speech recognition model, running in your browser. The first time, the model (about 77 MB) is downloaded and saved; after that it even works offline.

Does it work with WhatsApp voice notes?

Yes. Save the voice note (OGG or OPUS) or share it to the browser and choose it here. It also works with MP3, M4A, WAV and the audio of MP4, MOV or WebM videos.

How accurate is it?

With clear speech and little noise, very good. With lots of noise, several people talking at once or strong accents it makes more mistakes, which is why you can fix the text before downloading it.

How long does it take?

It depends on the device: on a computer a recording is usually transcribed in less time than it lasts; on a phone it can take longer. You can watch the text as it goes.

Can I create subtitles for a video?

Yes. Download the .srt or .vtt file with each sentence's timing and upload it to YouTube, your video editor or your player.
